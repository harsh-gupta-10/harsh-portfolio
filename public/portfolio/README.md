# Graphic Design & Video Editing Asset Directory

Place your creative works, images, and video thumbnails in these respective folders. They will be automatically referenced in your `/portfolio` route!

## Folder Mapping:

- `public/portfolio/posters/`
  - Put IT Educational weekly posters, college event posters, and tech infographics here.
  - Recommended format: `.png`, `.webp`, or `.jpg` (e.g. `poster-1.webp`, `tech-infographic-1.webp`).

- `public/portfolio/branding/`
  - Put Austrange Solutions logos, brand identity sheets, AI-to-Figma vector iterations, and brand graphics here.
  - (e.g. `austrange-logo-main.webp`, `austrange-concept.webp`).

- `public/portfolio/thumbnails/`
  - Put YouTube thumbnails from **IzooG10**, College Live Stream thumbnails (Annual Day, NEP Day, Farewell), and tech video covers here.
  - Recommended resolution: 1280x720 (16:9).

- `public/portfolio/social/`
  - Put social media posts, carousel slides, announcement graphics, and Instagram/LinkedIn creatives here.
  - Recommended ratio: 1:1 square or 4:5 vertical.

- `public/portfolio/videos/`
  - Put video stills, unboxing previews, live broadcast setup photos, and reel covers here.
